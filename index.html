A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s make
the vision clear.

Is GRC Grid available today?+

GRC Grid is in product definition and development. This site describes our intended enterprise offering. It does not represent shipped features, live connectors or a committed launch date.

Will every control be automated?+

No. We distinguish machine-verifiable controls, assisted assessments and manual reviews. Evidence freshness and coverage must be visible, and missing data must never be mistaken for a passing result.

How will the AI stay accountable?+

Through scoped permissions, source-grounded outputs, explicit approval boundaries and recorded tool actions. Formal attestations, risk acceptance and material changes stay with authorised people.

Can it work alongside our current GRC tools?+

Interoperability is part of the vision. A design partnership would establish the systems of record, integration scope and data ownership before implementing a connected workflow.

What does a design partnership look like?+

Start with a defined business area, a small control sample and relevant source systems. Agree ownership, data boundaries and acceptance criteria, then validate evidence quality and workflow outcomes before expanding.
